ADVENTIST HEALTH SYSTEM COMPUTER SCHOOL

This system manages student records and Microsoft Office training results for the school. It grades automatically, calculates final averages and produces professional, printable result certificates.

Grading Scale

75 – 100 Distinction
65 – 74 Credit
50 – 64 Pass
0 – 49 Fail

Final Grade = total of all 7 programs ÷ 7. It appears only when all seven scores are entered.

How it works

  1. Enter each student's grade per program.
  2. The system interprets the score automatically.
  3. When all 7 are in, the final grade is calculated.
  4. Print the certificate as an image, PDF or on paper.

Why this system?

  • Automatic grade interpretation and final average
  • Secure, role-based access to confidential results
  • Students view results with their ID and private PIN
  • Professional A4 certificates with watermark, date and signature line
  • Results appear for students as soon as they are entered
